What happens to your data?

What the bot keeps, what you can switch off, and how to take it with you.

What does the bot keep about me?

Your Telegram id and first name, your support requests, any wallet address you link or prove, your linked X account, its link codes and the links to posts you send for feedback, your wallet checks and privacy choices, any thanks, points or badges already on your account, a short trail of recent steps attached to a problem report, and the steps of any conversation in progress. If your team invites you to test as a reviewer, your test grades are kept too.

For the exact list on your own account, open My profile, then Account, then Your data, and tap See what is kept.

What are activity notes, and can I say no?

Activity notes are optional extra notes about actions such as joining, checking a wallet or filing a request. They hold ids and action details, not your name or message text, and they stay in the bot. They are on or off under Your data, and you can use the bot either way.

Bot improvements would let messages and button taps be used to improve the bot. That use has not started, so nothing is collected for it, whatever you choose.

Review practice lets reviewers your team invites see your X replies without your name. Turning it off removes those replies and their grades.

What do the technical logs hold?

Command names and button codes, when and in which chat something happened, message length, file types, which link opened the bot, posts and wallet checks, and the messages the bot sent. They contain no message text or secrets. The logs are kept whatever your settings are, and removed when you delete your account.

How do I get a copy of my data, or delete it?

Open Your data. Download my data sends you a file with your records. Delete my account asks you to confirm first, and you can also send /deleteaccount.

Deleting removes your saved name, wallet links and checks, X link, privacy choices, activity notes, posts, points, badges, the thanks you received and your logs. If you come back, you start as a new member.

What stays after I delete my account?

Thanks you gave stay with the people you gave them to, without your name. Support requests and spam-prevention records stay with the team. Backup copies remain until the backups expire. Messages or files already delivered in Telegram may remain there.

Who can see my requests and my posts?

Your community’s team. Support requests and X posts you send for review arrive in the team’s private chat, and everyone in that chat can read the names, messages and attachments on the cards. Reviewers your team invites can also see X replies without your name.

The bot never asks for a seed phrase, private key, password or login code, and deletes a message that looks like a private key or seed phrase.

What does this website store?

Only your choices, in your own browser: light or dark theme, language, and whether animations are paused. The site sets no cookies and uses no analytics.

Questions about your data?

Open the bot, tap Help, then Ask the team. A person answers in your private chat.
