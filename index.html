On X, paid posting drowned out the people who meant it.

A member takes the time to explain your project on X in their own words, because they use it and believe in it. For a while, apps scored people for posting about projects and rewarded the top scores. Accounts chasing points flooded the replies with AI-written answers, and the member who took the time to explain got buried. Creators who were paid to post often got little, depending on who took a cut in between.

On 15 January 2026 X’s head of product wrote that X would “no longer allow apps that reward users for posting on X”, and blamed AI slop and reply spam. Within a day, Kaito said it was shutting down Yaps, its score for posting about projects. A community that pays for posts now works against X’s own rules.

Anyone can become a professional and help pitch a project, out of loyalty or as a small business of their own. Both are fair when the work is real. But the method has to be forged, and that is where The Flock steps in.

We plan to let your project reward the members whose help your team confirmed, from its own share of the trading fee. Your project’s share goes straight to your wallet or contract, so nobody holds it or skims it on the way.

How The Flock works with X’s rule

  • Nobody is paid to post, to reply or to raid a cashtag.
  • We steer members away from raids and spam, because they don’t get a project the result it needs.

Answers that show where they come from.

A wrong answer that sounds sure does more harm than no answer. We’re testing these in private first.

  • Your team approves the texts it may answer from, such as your docs and the wording on your app’s pages. It answers only from those.
  • Every answer shows the exact passage it used and the date of that source, so members can check it themselves.
  • When the sources don’t cover a question, it says so and offers a person on your team.
  • When your team edits or withdraws a source, the bot stops answering from it.
  • During the test, a person reads every answer before it is sent.
