What happens when a member needs help, or meets a fake admin?

Support questions drown in a busy group, and scammers wait there posing as the team. The Flock moves each question into a private chat with your team.

🎫 Ask the team

Send a private request when you need help, want to raise a complaint or have an idea or proposal. A short set of questions helps the team understand what happened and what you need.

Never include a seed phrase or private key. Choose the topic closest to your request.
