These answers match ❓ Common questions under ❓ Help in the bot, with a little more detail.

Using The Flock

What can I do in The Flock?

You can ask the team for help, or practise replies for X and get feedback on them. The bot also keeps a record of what you contribute, so you can look back on it. The welcome card's buttons lead to each: 🛠 BUIDL, 👤 My profile and ❓ Help.

How do I get feedback on my posts on X?

Open 🛠 BUIDL, then 📣 Pitch, then ✍️ Get noticed on X. Before you post, ✍️ Check my reply looks at a draft and lists wording problems, such as a link, price talk or asking for likes. It doesn't keep your text, and nothing is published.

After you post, open 📤 My posts. The first time, you link your X account. The bot gives you a code that starts with FLOCK-. Post the code on X from your account, then send the bot the link to that post. The code works for 30 minutes, and the bot never asks for your password. From then on, 📤 Send a post takes a link to your own post from the last 48 hours. The bot tells you whether a person will review it. When they have, "📣 Your post feedback" arrives in your private chat with a grade: 👍 Good, ✏️ Needs work or 🚫 Not allowed.

Is posting on X paid?

No. Posting on X for the project is unpaid, and you can stop at any time. The bot says so on its BUIDL and Pitch screens.

How do I reach a person?

Open ❓ Help and tap 🎫 Ask the team. Choose 🛠 A problem, ⚠️ Complaint or 💡 Idea or proposal, then answer the short questions one message at a time. You can add files at the end before you tap ✅ Send request. The bot gives you a request number.

A person on the team replies in your private chat with the bot. Anything you type after sending is added to your latest request, and /status lists your requests and where each one stands.

Where can I see what I have contributed?

Open 👤 My profile and tap 📜 History. It lists, with dates, the requests the team marked useful, the posts it reviewed and the thanks you received and gave. 🏅 Badges shows any badges already on your account. New badge awards are paused for now.

Wallets and safety

Do I need a wallet?

No. You can get support and use Get noticed on X without one. If you want them, the optional wallet tools sit under 👤 My profile, then 🔐 Account.

🌊 Staking reads a public address you link and shows its TIDE staking position, such as the amount staked and when the 14-day lock ends. The staking itself happens on arctide.app. 🔐 Prove wallet shows that a wallet is yours. You send an exact small USDC amount from that wallet back to itself on Arc, approving the transfer in your own wallet. Do it within 30 minutes, then paste the transaction hash.

Is this message really from the team?

When someone says they are on the team, the bot can check their message for you. Open ❓ Help, then ❓ Common questions, then Wallets and safety, and tap 🛡 Is it really the team? Then forward the message to the bot within five minutes.

The bot answers in one of three ways: someone on the team wrote it, it doesn't know the person as part of the team, or it can't tell, because Telegram hides the writer of some messages. Names and screenshots can't be checked. The bot keeps neither the message nor who wrote it.

A yes still doesn't prove a message is safe, because a real team account can be taken over. And if your chat shows "Forwarded from", the person who sent it to you may not be on the team.

What will the team never ask me for?

The bot shows this promise on its welcome card, on Help and on every screen of the team check: "You never need to connect a wallet or pay to get support. Support replies arrive through this bot. We never ask for your seed phrase, private key, password or login code."

If you paste something that looks like a seed phrase or private key, the bot warns you and deletes the message, or asks you to delete it. If it was real, treat that wallet as exposed and move your funds to a new wallet.

Data and standing

What does the bot keep about me?

Your profile holds what the bot has about you, such as your support requests and the X posts you sent for feedback. For the full list, open 👤 My profile, then 🔐 Account, then 🔒 Your data, and tap 📋 See what is kept. 📥 Download my data sends you a file of your records.

Activity notes are optional. Turn them on or off under ⚙️ Optional settings, which you reach from 📋 See what is kept. Support and the requests you sent work the same either way.

What happens when I delete my account?

Open 🔒 Your data and tap 🗑 Delete my account, or send /deleteaccount. The bot lists what will go and asks you to confirm.

Deleting removes your saved name, your wallet and its proof, your X link, the posts you sent for review and your badges. Requests you sent and the spam-prevention records stay with the team, and backup copies remain until they expire. If you come back later, you start as a new member. The privacy page lists everything that goes and everything that stays.

What is Zeal?

Zeal is planned: a member’s standing, built from help that other people confirmed. It is not in the bot yet, so /tips shows no Zeal score and Thanks are paused.

How do I start?

Open the bot in Telegram and press Start.
