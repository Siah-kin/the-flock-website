About The Flock

Your whole community grows through the members who help it, yet their work goes unseen.

Why we built The Flock

In one token community we studied, ten members wrote more than half of about 8,000 messages in about three months. They answer newcomers, warn about scams and explain the project.

Almost half of that chat was greetings and price talk, so their help is hard to see. The tools teams use count messages. None of the tools we compared keeps a dated history of support and help your team confirmed that the member can download or delete.

We are building it for the OGs: the holders who stay loyal to a project beyond its price, and spend months teaching newcomers and pitching technology they believe in. Their heroics are rarely credited.

The problem with reward programmes

We studied 14 reward programmes. The ones that paid for visible activity, such as posts, quests or messages, got farmed.

The OGs end up below the farmers. When the programme or the project ends, the people who helped keep nothing to show for it.

Farming circles are nothing new. Bounty hunters and airdrop hunters were there long before AI. Everybody hated them, even if a wave of them was a sign the bull market was back. Either way, they nicked the rewards meant for the people who helped. Now AI runs the same game on steroids: it reverse engineers the rules and social engineers the people. That is a real threat to online communities, and that’s where The Flock steps in.

What The Flock does instead

The Flock counts something harder to fake: help that a person on your team confirmed. It goes on the member’s track record with the date. The track record belongs to them, and they can download it.

What else goes on a track record, and can it be deleted?
  • Reviewed X posts go on it too.
  • Members can delete their track record from the bot.

The AI we want

A lot of the internet has become a poor place to spend time. AI writes much of what we read, and bots farm the replies, so the people who actually explain a project get buried.

The good AI is the AI you don’t notice: AI in the human loop, making the human experience more intuitive and preventing the pitfalls that have been choking society. We want it to boost human creativity instead of farming it. It should help a person say what they mean in their own words, and point out what reads as farmed.

Who is behind it

Built by a founder who ran a crypto business until 2025.

My crypto business went bankrupt in 2025, after the long bear market in altcoins. All my work was washed away. The help I gave and the results I delivered were scattered in chats nobody uses any more.

Looking for a new job was close to impossible. Former business owners like me are not favoured; we read as overqualified.

Real work in a community should leave a track record that outlives the job, the project and the market cycle, and that belongs to the person who did it. So we are building that track record: who actually helped, confirmed by the people they helped.
