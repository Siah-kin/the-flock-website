What this is

The Flock is a Telegram bot for the Arctide community. Members can ask the team for support, check a draft before posting on X, and see activity the bot connects to their account.

Useful work can be easy to forget. The bot gives members a dated History entry when the team marks a problem report Useful. Team members who review reports do not receive a History entry for that work today.

This page explains what the bot does today and what the team is still exploring.

Why we do it this way

The bot is already used by the Arctide community for support requests, Get noticed on X and My profile. This site describes those features alongside ideas that are still being explored.

Points and a reward pool are not live. Their rules, including how a team confirms useful work and how to prevent farming, have not been set.

Why we already know this is needed

Good things get built around real people. You learn what they need by spending time with them and watching closely, not by guessing in a meeting. Most projects skip that part. It is slow, and it does not look impressive.

We spent seven years on it. Running real online communities by hand, every day. Watching what helped people and what let them down.

So we are not guessing about the need. We saw it, up close, for years. The slow part is the strength. You cannot design something like this from a whiteboard.

What work does the bot recognise today?

When someone reports a bug, typo or broken step, the team receives a numbered card in its chat. If a team member closes it as Useful, the reporter sees a dated entry in History.

This gives the reporter a dated History entry. The team member who reviewed it does not get an entry for that work today.

We are exploring ways to recognise more useful community work. What would qualify, how decisions would be checked, and how to prevent farming still need to be worked out.

Useful work can be easy to forget. The History entry makes the team’s decision about a report visible to the member who sent it.

Spot a problem and report it

Found a bug, typo or broken step? In The Flock, open Help → Ask the team → A problem and send a report.

The bot sends it to the team chat as a numbered card. A team member can review it and close it as Useful. When they do, the report appears in your History with the date.

What happens after a report?

If the team marks your report Useful, the bot adds a dated entry to your History. That is recognition of the report. It does not pay you or award points.

The team member who reviews a report does not get a History entry for that review today.

What is available today?

Here is what happens in the bot now:

Report: Open Help, choose Ask the team, then A problem. Your report goes to the team chat as a numbered card.

Team review: A team member can close the card as Useful. The bot then adds a dated entry to the reporter’s History.

No rewards are attached: Bug reports do not earn a payout, points or a share of a pool today.

Planned rewards are not live: there is no reward amount or start date to promise. The History entry shows the team’s decision; it is not a payment.

What comes next

The report and review flow is available today. Points and a reward pool are planned, but they are not live. There is no launch date or reward amount to promise.

Any future reward system would need clear rules for what counts and how the team confirms it before it could be described as available.

For now, reporting a problem can add a dated Useful entry to your History. It does not earn a reward.

How to report a problem

Open The Flock in Telegram, choose Help → Ask the team → A problem, and describe the bug, typo or broken step.

Your report goes to the team chat as a numbered card. If the team closes it as Useful, you will see a dated entry in your History. Reporting does not earn points or money.

Try it in the bot. Choose Help, then Ask the team, then A problem.
