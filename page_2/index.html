Why does The Flock exist?

Because a lot of good work in a community goes unseen, and the tools meant to reward it get farmed.

What is wrong with the internet today?

A lot of the internet has become a poor place to spend time. AI writes much of what we read, and bots farm the replies, so the people who explain a project well get buried.

Who gets forgotten?

The OGs: holders who stay loyal to a project beyond its price, and spend months teaching newcomers and pitching technology they believe in. Quest platforms and raid bots get reverse engineered by farming circles, so farming accounts climb the board and the OGs end up below them.

What about paying people to post?
  • On 15 January 2026 X said it would no longer allow apps that reward users for posting.
  • The Flock pays nobody to post, reply or raid a cashtag.
  • Reward points and a pool are planned, with no amount or date set yet.

What do we want to do about it?

The Flock counts something harder to fake: help that a person on your team confirmed.

We also want AI to boost human creativity instead of farming it. It should help a person say what they mean in their own words, and point out what reads as farmed. The AI coach that does this is planned.

What does it look like when someone helps?

A member finds a bug, a typo or a broken step and reports it in the bot. It reaches your team chat as a numbered ticket. When your team taps Useful, it goes on that member’s track record with the date.

What else goes on a track record?
  • Reviewed X posts and thanks between members go on it too.
  • The team member who reviewed a report gets no entry today.

Try it as a member.

Open the bot and press Start to see it as your members do. Then tap Help, Ask the team, A problem.
