How do you use The Flock?

Open the bot in Telegram and press Start.

Members ask your team for help in private and get feedback on their X posts. Your team answers both from one private group chat.

For members

  • How do I ask my team for support?
  • How do I check a message is really from my team?
  • How do I check a reply before I post it on X?
  • Where is my track record?

  1. How do I ask my team for support?

    Your request reaches your team in private, away from strangers in the group. Tap Help, then Ask the team. Pick a problem, a complaint or an idea and send it. Your request gets a number, and the answer arrives in your private chat with the bot.

    More about requests
    • Send /status to see where your requests stand.
    • You can attach a screenshot before you send.
    • Never include a seed phrase or private key.

  2. How do I check a message is really from my team?

    The bot can tell you whether a message came from someone on your team’s admin list. Forward the message to the bot. If Telegram hides who wrote it, the bot says it can’t tell.

    The bot first asks where the message should go: tap Check who wrote it, then forward the message again within five minutes. The same check is in Help, under Common questions, then Wallets and safety: tap Is it really the team? and forward the message. Names and screenshots can’t be checked, so forward the message itself.

    What the bot will never ask for
    • Your seed phrase, private key, password or login code.
    • A wallet connection or a payment to get support.
    • If you paste something that looks like a private key, the bot deletes it.

  3. How do I check a reply before I post it on X?

    Before you post a reply on X, the bot points out ten things that often get a reply skipped or hidden. It keeps no copy of your draft.

    Open BUIDL, then Pitch, then Get noticed on X, then Check my reply, and paste your draft.

    After you post, send the link under My posts, and someone on your team gives you feedback in private.

    What Check my reply looks for
    • A reply under 20 characters, or over 280
    • A link that starts with http or www
    • The $TIDE ticker, the name Arctide or a contract address that starts with 0x
    • More than two hashtags, or more than one cashtag besides $TIDE
    • Mostly capital letters
    • Price talk or a promised gain
    • Income or legal claims
    • Asking for likes or reposts
    • About ten stock phrases, and two rockets in a row
    • Writing that sounds like AI: long dashes, “not this but that”, a chatbot opener or closer, and words AI tools overuse. Two of these together raise the remark

    The bot shows six remarks at most. The Reply guide shows how to write replies people answer.

  4. Where is my track record?

    Your track record is a dated list of the requests your team marked useful and the posts it reviewed. To see it, open My profile, then History.

    To take your data with you, open My profile, then Account, then Your data, and tap Download my data.

    What the bot keeps about you
    • Your support requests and the X posts you sent for feedback.
    • Open My profile, then Account, then Your data, and tap See what is kept for the full list.
    • Deleting your account removes your name, your wallet and X links, your posts and your badges. Requests you sent stay with the team, so the team still has the requests it marked useful.

For your team

The Flock runs in Arctide’s community today. To bring it to your community, send us your idea: under Help, tap Ask the team, then Idea or proposal.

  • How do we plan our community?
  • How do we set up our team chat?
  • How do we answer requests?
  • How do we grade posts?

  1. How do we plan our community?

    Answer seven short questions and the guide gives you a plan for your community, with a next step. The guide opens for people on your team’s admin list: open Guides in the bot and tap Plan your community.

    More about the plan
    • The questions follow four steps: Your idea, Your people, Do something together and Listen and improve.
    • You can change any answer before you finish.
    • The plan stays in your chat with the bot and is not posted to your community.

  2. How do we set up our team chat?

    Your team sees every request and post for review in one place: the team chat. Someone on your team’s admin list sets it up: make a private Telegram group for your team and add the bot. Everyone in the group can read members’ names and messages, so add only your team.

  3. How do we answer requests?

    Each request arrives in the team chat as a numbered card, and you answer it from the card. Tap Reply, and your answer goes to the member’s private chat. Tap Close, then choose Done, Useful or Spam. Useful puts the help on the member’s track record.

    More about the team chat
    • The bot holds likely spam back from the team chat, so real requests come first. /noise lists what it held, and you can release anything real.
    • A tap on Spam closes the request without telling the sender and gives them a strike. After two strikes, their next request is held too. A report of a hack or drained funds still reaches the team.
    • Every Monday the team chat gets an overview: who did the work and what is still waiting. /overview shows it any time.

  4. How do we grade posts?

    Your team gives members feedback on their X posts, in private. A member links their X account under My posts and sends the link to a post they published. Someone on the team picks Good, Needs work or Not allowed, and gives a reason for anything other than Good.

    Send /review in the team chat to get the next post waiting, then read it on X and grade it.

Common questions

Is posting on X paid?

Posting is your choice. Nobody is paid to post, to reply or to raid a cashtag, and you can stop at any time.

Do I need a wallet?

Support and the X tools work without one. Some communities switch on token tools, such as a staking view, which read a public address you choose to link.

Is a “yes” from the team check a guarantee?

It tells you the account is on your team’s admin list. A real team account can still be taken over, so stay careful with any message that asks you to act fast or to send something.

How do I delete my account?

Open My profile, then Account, then Your data, and tap Delete my account, or send /deleteaccount. The bot lists what will go and asks you to confirm.

Does The Flock replace our moderation bot?

Keep the bot you use to moderate your group. The Flock works alongside it, in private chats and your team chat.
