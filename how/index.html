How to use The Flock

The Flock is Arctide's community bot on Telegram. Through it you can ask the team for help, practise replies for X and get the team's feedback on what you post. Each contribution the team confirms is added to your own record. You use it in a private chat at @Arctide_TheFlock_Bot.

The bot makes you one promise about safety:

You never need to connect a wallet or pay to get support. Support replies arrive through this bot. We never ask for your seed phrase, private key, password or login code.

This page walks through every screen in the order you meet them. Inside the bot, a short tour with examples opens from ❓ Help > 📖 How it works, or when you type /howitworks. Common questions sit under ❓ Help in the bot, and our FAQ page has more answers. When none of that covers it, Ask the team sends your question to a person, and the reply comes back in your private chat.

Early version. The bot is young. A few parts are paused or still being built, such as new badge awards, and the screen in question says so when you reach it.

For members

Everything a member does happens in a private chat with the bot, and every reply from the team arrives there too.

Wallet proof and staking

Both wallet screens are optional. You can get support and use Get noticed on X without either, and the bot never connects to your wallet.

🔐 Prove wallet (or /prove) checks that a wallet is under your control, which an address alone can't show. You paste your public address. The bot then asks you to send an exact small amount of USDC from that wallet to the same wallet on Arc, within 30 minutes. It costs only the network fee, and the USDC stays in your wallet. You approve the transfer in your own wallet, then paste its transaction hash into the chat. A private key looks much like a hash, so check what you copy before you send it.

🌊 Staking (or /staking) reads the $TIDE staking position of a public address you link with /wallet. It shows what you have staked, when the 14-day lock ends, the TIDE and USDC ready to claim, and the pool's totals. 🔄 Refresh reads it again, and a reminders button turns notifications on or off. Staking itself happens on arctide.app, which 🌐 Open arctide.app ↗ opens. Linking an address doesn't prove you own it or move any funds, and /unlink removes it.

Is it really the team?

In crypto groups, people sometimes pretend to be the team. If someone sends you a message in Telegram and you're not sure it's genuine, the bot can check who wrote it. Open ❓ Help > ❓ Common questions > Wallets and safety > 🛡 Is it really the team?, then forward the message within five minutes. The bot tells you whether someone on the team wrote it, that it doesn't know the person, or that it can't tell, for example when Telegram hides the writer. It forgets the message and its writer once it has answered. Even a real team account can be taken over, so refuse anyone who asks for your seed phrase, your private key or a payment.

For the community team

Members' requests and X posts reach the team as cards in a private Telegram group, the team chat. You answer there or from your own private chat with the bot, and the member reads your answer in their private chat.

Who counts as the team

Only people on the bot's admin list can use the team tools. The bot's owner keeps that list in the bot's server settings, and there is no Telegram command for adding people yet. To be added, ask the bot's owner. Reading the team chat and answering through the bot are separate permissions. Everyone in the group can read the cards, and people on the admin list answer them.

Setting up the team chat

Requests carry members' names, messages and files, so the team chat must be a private group with only the people who should read them. If you're on the admin list, press Start in your private chat with the bot. With no team chat yet, it shows "First, set up a private team chat" and the button ➕ Set up my team chat.

Held-back requests

The bot holds back requests that look like spam, such as random or repeated text. They don't appear as cards, and your team desk shows them as held back. Send /noise in the team chat to see them, then tap ✅ Release to post the card or 🗑 Dismiss to remove it. A dismissal counts against the sender, and /forgive clears a member's strikes.

Reviewing X posts

When a member sends a post for feedback, a review card arrives in the team chat. By then the bot has already checked that the X account is theirs and that the post is from the last 48 hours. The weekly brief

The brief is a short line the team writes for members each week. Send /brief followed by your text, up to 500 characters, and it appears on every member's Home card after "This week on X:". /brief off clears it, and /brief on its own reminds you how to set it. The brief is text only.

The carousel

The carousel posts your team's announcements and reminders in the community group, one at a time, every few hours, so nobody has to remember them. The team writes the posts in a private Telegram channel, and every admin of that channel can write and change them. Keep the channel separate from the team chat, because members' requests don't belong in published posts.

  1. Make a private channel for your team's posts.
  2. Add the bot to the channel as an admin.
  3. In your private chat with the bot, send /content. The bot gives you a code.
  4. In the channel, post /content and the code. The bot tells you when they are paired.

From then on, each new post in the channel joins the carousel. The bot posts only in a community group it has just confirmed is private, and it never posts in a public group. /carousel shows the settings. Posts can't be given an end date yet.

Troubleshooting

The bot is quiet in our group

In a public group, the bot sends no welcome and /thanks does nothing. /start there only points you to the private chat. Open your private chat with @Arctide_TheFlock_Bot and press Start. Everything on this page works there. In a private group, the bot welcomes new members with buttons that open the private chat. There, /thanks answers that thanks are paused.

I can't find Ask the team

The welcome card mentions Ask the team but has no button with that name. Tap ❓ Help, then 🎫 Ask the team, or type /ticket.

Where is the team's reply?

Replies arrive in your private chat with the bot, so scroll up in that chat. /status lists your requests and where each one stands. The team replies when they can, and the bot doesn't promise a response time.

The bot says my reply "drops the ticker", but I only wrote Arctide

Check my reply counts the plain word Arctide as a ticker for now. If that is the only remark, you can ignore it.

Wallet proof asks for a hash

The transaction hash is the id of your self-transfer. Copy it from your wallet's activity list or a block explorer. It starts with 0x. Never paste a private key or seed phrase, which can look similar. If the 30 minutes run out, start again with /prove. Proof is optional, and you can leave it.

I proved my wallet and got no badge

New badge awards are paused, so proving a wallet doesn't add one right now. Badges already on your account stay there.

I'm stuck halfway through a step

Type /cancel to stop whatever step is waiting, then /start to go back to Home.
