How do you use The Flock?

Open the bot in Telegram and press Start.

For members

How do I ask my team for support?

Tap Help, then Ask the team. Pick a problem, a complaint or an idea and send it. Your request gets a number, and the answer arrives in your private chat with the bot.

More about requests
  • Send /status to see where your requests stand.
  • You can attach a screenshot before you send.
  • Never include a seed phrase or private key.

How do I check a message is really from my team?

Forward the message to the bot. It tells you whether someone on your team wrote it. Names and screenshots can’t be checked, so forward the message itself.

What the bot will never ask for
  • Your seed phrase, private key, password or login code.
  • A wallet connection or a payment to get support.
  • If you paste something that looks like a private key, the bot deletes it.

How do I check a reply before I post it on X?

Open BUIDL, then Get noticed on X, then Check my reply, and paste your draft. The bot points out what often gets replies hidden, such as a link, a ticker or price talk. It keeps no copy of your draft.

More about posting on X
  • The Reply guide shows how to write replies people answer.

How do I get feedback on a post?

Link your X account under My posts, then send the link to a post you published. Someone on your team grades it Good, Needs work or Not allowed, and says why. The feedback arrives in your private chat.

Where is my track record?

Open My profile, then History. It lists, with dates, the requests your team marked useful and the posts it reviewed. The track record is yours: download it or delete it under Your data.

What the bot keeps about you
  • Your support requests and the X posts you sent for feedback.
  • Open My profile, then Account, then Your data, and tap See what is kept for the full list.
  • Deleting your account removes your name, your links, your posts and your badges. Requests you sent stay with the team.

For your team

How do we set up our team chat?

Make a private Telegram group for your team and add the bot. That group becomes your team chat, where every request and post for review arrives. Keep it private: everyone in it can read members’ names and messages.

How do we answer requests?

Each request arrives as a numbered card. Tap Reply, and your answer goes to the member’s private chat. Close it as Done, Useful or Spam. Useful puts the help on the member’s track record.

More about the team chat
  • Likely spam waits in its own list, so real requests come first.
  • Every Monday the team chat gets a summary of who helped and what is still waiting. /overview shows it any time.

How do we grade members’ posts?

Send /review in the team chat to get the next post waiting. Read it on X, grade it, and add a reason. The member gets your feedback in private.

Common questions

Is posting on X paid?

Posting is your choice. Nobody is paid to post, to reply or to raid a cashtag, and you can stop at any time.

Do I need a wallet?

Support and the X tools work without one. Some communities switch on token tools, such as a staking view, which read a public address you choose to link.

Is a “yes” from the team check a guarantee?

It tells you the account is on your team. A real team account can still be taken over, so stay careful with any message that asks you to act fast or to send something.

How do I delete my account?

Open My profile, then Account, then Your data, and tap Delete my account, or send /deleteaccount. The bot lists what will go and asks you to confirm.

Does The Flock replace our moderation bot?

Keep the bot you use to moderate your group. The Flock works alongside it, in private chats and your team chat.
