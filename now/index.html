Where is The Flock now, and what comes next?

What members can use today, what we are testing, and what we are building next.

What can members use today?

Members ask your team for support in private, check their X drafts before they post, and build a dated track record of the help your team confirms.

What works today
  • Numbered private requests, answered from one team chat
  • A check for fake admins, and a bot that never asks for a seed phrase
  • Check my reply, the reply guide, and your team grading members’ posts
  • A dated History that members can download or delete
  • The weekly brief and scheduled posts

The full list, with links to the manual

What are we testing?

Members can go from a post worth answering to X in one tap, reply in their own words, and come back to the bot for your team’s feedback.

Also in testing
  • A wider check for AI phrasing in members’ drafts
  • An overview for your team: who did the work, and what is still waiting
  • Training for trusted members who grade posts
  • Problem reports that carry the bot’s own log of what went wrong

What are we building next?

We are building an AI coach that helps members write in their own words. Also planned: points for help your team confirmed, and a reward pool your project funds from part of its trading fees.

More about the plans
  • Zeal: a member’s standing, built from help that other people confirmed
  • Your own version of The Flock, under your community’s name
