Where is The Flock now, and what comes next?

What can members use today?

Members ask your team for support in private, check their X drafts before they post, and keep a dated track record of the help your team confirms.

What works today
  • Numbered private requests, answered from one team chat
  • A Monday overview in your team chat: who did the work and what is still waiting. /overview shows it any time
  • A check for fake admins, and a bot that never asks for a seed phrase
  • Check my reply, the reply guide, and your team grading members’ posts
  • A dated History, with a data download and account deletion for members
  • Posts for members to answer on X, chosen by your team
  • A slideshow of your team’s channel posts, shared in your private community group once you set it up
  • A guided plan for your community, in Guides, for the team’s listed admins. Starting out? Send us your idea through Help, Ask the team, Idea or proposal.

How each part works

What are we testing?

Members can go from a post worth answering to X in one tap, reply in their own words, and come back to the bot for your team’s feedback.

Also in testing
  • Training for trusted members who grade posts
  • Problem reports that carry the bot’s own log of what went wrong

In development

Answers your team can check

  • The assistant answers only from texts your team has approved. A member’s answer joins them once that member says OK and someone on your team approves it.
  • Every answer shows the passage it came from, and that text’s date.
  • When your texts don’t cover a question, the question goes to a person on your team.
  • Edit or withdraw a text, and the assistant stops using the old version.
Also in development
  • A link on this website that opens the bot and asks a founder two questions about their idea.
  • Changes to the bot’s menus, including a new name for BUIDL: Contribute.

Planned

What comes next?

An AI coach helps members write in their own words. Members get points for help your team confirmed. The points already in a member’s History come from an earlier version of the bot.

More on what comes next
  • Zeal: a member’s standing, built from help that other people confirmed
  • Your own version of The Flock, under your community’s name
  • Proofs that confirm something about a member without exposing the details

How Arctide splits your token’s trading fees

If your token trades on Arctide, every trade pays a fee in USDC, and you set part of the split.

Your project’s share goes straight to your wallet or contract, so nobody holds it or skims it on the way.

Planned

Your project sets aside part of its fee share as a reward pool. Members whose help your team confirmed claim from it with their points. Posts on X never add points, and nothing is paid per post.

An example that works today: Arctide’s own token, TIDE. Its stakers claim TIDE and USDC on arctide.app, and The Flock shows each member’s position.
